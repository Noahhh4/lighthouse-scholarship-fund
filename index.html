About the Fund

Supporting rural students since 2007

The Lighthouse Scholarship Fund was established in 2007 by Jenny Munro to ensure talented students from the Far South Coast aren't held back by geography or financial barriers.

Our Purpose

Financial support and mentorship for students pursuing tertiary education. We help cover tuition, accommodation, and living expenses so students can focus on their studies.

Our Community

Founded by Jenny Munro, who also established Lighthouse Surgery Narooma and Bermagui Medical Centre, continuing a legacy of community support.

Our Impact

Many scholars return to bring valuable skills back to the region. Our graduates work in healthcare, education, business, and community services across the Far South Coast.

How to Apply

Our application process is designed to be straightforward and accessible.

01

Review Eligibility

Check if you're eligible. Applicants must be from the Far South Coast region of NSW and enrolled in or planning to commence tertiary education.

02

Prepare Your Application

Gather your academic transcripts, write a personal statement explaining your goals, and arrange references from teachers or community members who know you well.

03

Submit Your Application

Complete the online application form and upload all required documents before the deadline. Make sure everything is accurate and complete.

04

Selection Process

One student is selected each year based on their application. Successful applicants receive financial support and ongoing mentorship throughout their studies.
