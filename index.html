Our Mission

The Lighthouse Scholarship Fund supports students from Narooma and surrounding regions to pursue tertiary education. We provide more than financial assistance - we offer guidance, encouragement, and a strong community.

Each year, capable and motivated young people in our community face financial and geographic barriers to higher education. Through targeted financial support and a strong local network, we help students take the next step with confidence.

How It Works

🎓

For Students

Who Can Apply?

  • Residents of Narooma or surrounding regions
  • Enrolled, or planning to enrol, in tertiary education
  • Commitment to community engagement
  • Demonstrated financial need

What We Offer

  • Financial scholarships for tuition and living expenses
  • One-on-one mentorship from alumni and professionals
  • Connection to our scholar community
  • Career guidance and networking opportunities

Application Process

Applications open annually in [Month]. Contact us or your school for eligibility requirements, deadline, and application materials.

💙

For Donors

Why Give?

Your generosity directly supports students in our region to access higher education. Every dollar contributes directly to a student's education and helps build a stronger, more educated community on the Far South Coast.

Ways to Contribute

  • One-time donations of any amount
  • Annual scholarship sponsorship
  • Planned giving and bequests
  • Corporate partnerships

Your Impact

100% of your donations go to support students. We keep you updated on the impact that you make on students.

Get Involved Beyond Giving

  • Become a mentor to current scholars
  • Share career opportunities with our network
  • Volunteer at scholarship events

Ready to Make a Difference?

Whether you're a student with dreams or a supporter who wants to help, we'd love to hear from you.
