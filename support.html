Why your support matters.

The Lighthouse Scholarship Fund relies on the generosity of individuals, businesses, and organisations who believe that where you grow up shouldn't limit how far you can go.

Every contribution — large or small — goes directly toward supporting students from the Far South Coast with the financial assistance they need to pursue higher education and build meaningful careers.

Financial

Make a donation

One-off or recurring donations of any amount help us support more students each year. Get in touch to discuss how your contribution can make the biggest impact.

Mentorship

Become a mentor

Share your professional experience with our scholars. Mentors play a vital role in helping students navigate university life and plan their careers.

Partnerships

Partner with us

Businesses and organisations can partner with the fund to sponsor scholarships, provide internships, or offer employment pathways for our graduates.

We'd love to hear from you. Reach out and we'll get back to you promptly.
