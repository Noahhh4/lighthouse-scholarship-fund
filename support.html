Get involved

Support the fund.

Every contribution makes a difference.

Students from regional NSW face barriers their city peers simply don't: the cost of relocating, the distance from family, the lack of local networks. The Lighthouse Scholarship Fund exists to remove those barriers.

There are a number of ways to get involved, whether financially or through your time and expertise. The Fund holds Deductible Gift Recipient (DGR) status, so donations over $2 are tax deductible.

Financial

Make a donation

One-off or ongoing donations of any size go directly toward tuition, accommodation, and living costs for our scholars. Get in touch to find out more.

Mentorship

Share your professional experience with a scholar navigating university life and early career decisions. Mentors are among the most valued contributors to the fund.
