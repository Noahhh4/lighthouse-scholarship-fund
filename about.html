Removing barriers for regional students.

Founder photo
Our founders

Jock & Dr Jenny Munro

Our children had graduated and were independent and in 2001 we agreed to provide on-campus accommodation for our niece when she gained entry to Flinders University. Helen embraced the opportunity and was offered a scholarship in her third year.

The following year we offered the first of our scholarships to a student from Narooma High School. Our accountant suggested that we establish a charity and hence the Lighthouse Scholarship Fund was formed.

Since then the Fund has supported thirty-eight students as well as providing over $100,000 to a literacy program at the Narooma Public School, $80,000 to Little Yuin Pre-School and voyages for four young people on the Young Endeavour through the Order of Australia Association.

It would be gratifying to receive tax deductible donations from other regions in NSW so that we can offer more scholarships.

Est. 2007 — Narooma, NSW

A small fund with a big belief.

Jock and Jenny started the fund with a simple conviction: that talent exists everywhere, and opportunity shouldn't be limited by postcode. If that resonates, there's a place for you here.
