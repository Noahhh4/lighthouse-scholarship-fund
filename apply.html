Application process

How to apply.

Application form

There's no points score or academic cut-off — we want to understand who you are, where you come from, and what you hope to achieve. Fill out the form and attach anything that helps tell your story.

Who can apply
Students from regional NSW planning to undertake tertiary education, at any stage of their journey.
What we look for
Drive, character, and a genuine connection to the regional NSW community.
Applications open
Annually. Contact us for the current round's closing date.
Not sure?
Email jenny@jmunro.com.au before applying. We're happy to have a chat.

Supporting documents
Attach files

Transcripts, a resume, letters of reference, a personal statement. Anything that helps us understand who you are. You can select multiple files.

Accepted formats: PDF, Word, images. Max 10 MB per file. Having trouble? Email jenny@jmunro.com.au.
